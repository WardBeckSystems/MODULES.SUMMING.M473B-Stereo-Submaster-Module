> Hello.
>
> Well you do NOT require those transformers. That board that he is selling/in possession of was one of two ever made. Your modules have a special daughter board that can and should be 100% removed. The transformer was for that daughter board. The console that he has/you have was for a client that used the L module a little differently. It had this daughter board as an additional line amplifyer to send signal to a Front of House (FOH) board, in another building for monitoring. The transformers took the actively balanced signal (common with WBS) and went through the extra transformer to drive the long cable run, thus increasing protection, isolation, etc..... The other board that was its sister console used the transformers at a 600 ohm impedance to run tape machines. And for the record I purchased two (2) of these very modules from Dan a few years ago, and use them regularly today.
>
> There are a few boards out there that have transformers on the out-put of each channel, sub mix groupe, mains, and I believe on the sends too. I have only seen one in my life, but Pathe in France has the worlds longest console, NBC has a couple, CBC of course, and a few others from days gone by.
>
> So enjoy your modules, remove the additional daughter board, and don't worry about the transformers (maybe make them switchable on the out put so they are there if you need them for isolation or not).
>
> Regards,
